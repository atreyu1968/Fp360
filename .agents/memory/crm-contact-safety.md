---
name: CRM contact safety
description: Privacy and simulation boundaries for contact records and CRM follow-ups.
---

CRM contact records are for fictional classroom scenarios. They may keep a simulated reference person's name, role and permitted practice channel, but must not store personal phone numbers or email addresses. Calls, meetings and follow-ups are audit-only simulations; the app never sends them to real people.

**Why:** RED FP360 is an educational simulation and its classroom materials require avoiding collection or exposure of minors' personal contact details.

**How to apply:** future CRM forms, exports and integrations must preserve this boundary. Do not add personal contact channels or external messaging without a clear approved change to scope and privacy review.
