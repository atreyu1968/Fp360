---
name: CRM contact safety
description: Privacy and simulation boundaries for contact records and CRM follow-ups.
---

CRM contact records are for fictional classroom scenarios. They may keep a simulated reference person's name, role and permitted practice channel, but must not store personal phone numbers or email addresses. Calls, meetings and follow-ups are audit-only simulations; the app never sends them to real people. External mentors may see only their assigned anonymized cases and collaboration records, never student dossiers or named attendance; shared reports remain aggregated.

**Why:** RED FP360 is an educational simulation. Its classroom materials require avoiding collection or exposure of minors' personal contact details, and the mentorship dossier limits external access to protect student records.

**How to apply:** future CRM and mentorship forms, exports and integrations must preserve this boundary. Do not add personal contact channels, student-level mentor access, or external messaging without a clear approved change to scope and privacy review.
