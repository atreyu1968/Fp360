---
name: RED FP360 company lifecycle
description: Product rules for center-owned companies, onboarding, access scopes, and fiscal calendars.
---

A center may have multiple active simulated companies. Onboarding must offer continuing with an existing company without duplicating it, creating a blank company, or creating a company from a reviewable AI proposal containing fictional, non-personal initial data. AI-generated proposals require teacher approval; they must not create untraceable transactions or bypass accounting and operational controls.

Access is scoped by center permissions: center-level managers may manage the center's company portfolio, while teachers only access companies assigned to them. Center access must never imply network-wide access.

Each company can use either continuous fiscal years that persist across academic courses, or an accelerated virtual calendar that lets a class complete a 12-month exercise during a course. Academic courses and accounting fiscal years are separate; course completion must not force a fiscal-year close. Store the academic period per company. Calendar mode and fiscal-year start month determine its economic network, so companies in the same center can use different network calendars without sharing transactions across incompatible networks.

**Why:** On 3 November 2026, the user confirmed multiple companies per center, the three onboarding paths, center-permission-based access, both fiscal-calendar modes configurable per company, and strict isolation between ordinary and accelerated economic networks.

**How to apply:** Keep the selected company and center authorization consistent across modules. Store onboarding provenance and review actions. Keep generated seed data synthetic and auditable. Bind each company to a compatible simulation network, reject cross-network transactions and source links, and scope AI context and proposals to one network at a time while including all its companies and operations. Keep accounting calendars independent from academic dates and make annual closing an explicit teacher-led action.
