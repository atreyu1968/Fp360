---
name: RED FP360 labor and payroll safeguards
description: Fictional-data and non-filing boundaries for HR, payroll, and social-security modules.
---

Keep labor and payroll workflows strictly educational: use fictional employee and candidate references, do not collect real identity, contact, health, or bank-account data, and do not submit filings or payments to external agencies. Payroll rates must be instructor-configured, versioned scenario parameters, never presented as statutory or current rates. Contract, receipt, and contribution records are training summaries, not official documents.

**Why:** The RED FP360 dossiers define a practice environment using fictional companies and people; the workflows must not be mistaken for real employment administration.

**How to apply:** Preserve server-generated pseudonymous references, deterministic calculations from clearly labeled educational parameters, separate audited payroll stages, explicit simulation/no-validity notices, and links to official information rather than live integrations or official-form replicas.
