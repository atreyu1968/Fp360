---
name: RED FP360 evaluation scope
description: Curriculum mapping, scoring, privacy, and audit boundaries for the teacher-evaluation module.
---

Keep learning-outcome and criterion codes configurable and versioned per campaign; do not hardcode one universal curriculum mapping across cycles or centers. Rubric scores, recoveries, and group closure are didactic records only, not official grades or acts. Every assessment must point to validated portfolio evidence, use a published rubric, and append a separate attempt for recovery. Do not let AI assign grades. Restrict named results to the student's authorized teacher and the student; team managers receive aggregate summaries only.

**Why:** The official module 23 dossier states curriculum mappings differ by cycle and center, individual results must not leak between students, and professional judgment must remain with educators.

**How to apply:** When extending module 23 or connecting it to other RED FP360 modules, preserve campaign-scoped curriculum versions, evidence-backed assessment, append-only history, didactic disclaimers, and server-enforced role/organization boundaries.
